SMART Homework Goal Strategy

This app is structured specifically around the SMART goal criteria to make homework stress-free and rewarding.

S Specific

Complete all assigned daily homework assignments (Math, Reading, Science, History) by breaking them into manageable single quests.

M Measurable

Track progress through the completion ratio (0/5 weekly quests) and earned coin totals.

A Achievable

Use 25-minute Pomodoro focus blocks and constant time announcements to prevent burnout and keep work manageable.

R Relevant

Connect homework completion directly to real-life motivating prizes (gaming time, dessert trips, privileges) chosen by parents and kids.

T Time-Bound

Complete homework by 6:00 PM every school day using 30-minute interval chime broadcasts as pacing reminders.
