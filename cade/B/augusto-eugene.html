Post-School Buffer Zone

Just got home? Don't hit the bed yet.

School drained your battery. Scrolling in bed feels relaxing, but it actually leaves you more exhausted and stressed later. Take 3 minutes for this transition ritual instead.

1

Change Out of School Clothes & Drink Water

Physical signal to your brain that school is done. Hydration offsets afternoon brain fog.

2

Set Phone in Another Room or Face Down

If it's in your hand, algorithms win. Put it out of arm's reach while you decompress.

3

Sit at Desk or Table (Not Bed!)

Bed is for sleep. Sitting upright keeps your energy awake for just 15-20 minutes of quick work.

Goal: Zero guilt. Just 5 minutes of low-effort start.
The "Rule of 5 Minutes"

Commit to ONLY 5 Minutes

If you still feel too tired after 5 minutes, you have permission to stop. The hardest part is just starting!
